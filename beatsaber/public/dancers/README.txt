DROP REAL ANIME ARTWORK HERE
============================

The background dancers fall back to drawn silhouettes because
there is no artwork in this folder. Put real sprites here and
they are used instead, automatically.

1. Put the image files in this folder.
   Animated .gif and animated .webp both work and will play.
   .png works for a still figure.

   Transparent backgrounds look far better than a solid box.

2. List them in manifest.json, exactly like this:

   ["dancer1.gif", "dancer2.gif", "dancer3.png"]

3. Reload the page.

That is all. The parade still applies: they walk in from the
side, stay for about 13 seconds, and leave, scaled and placed
at random toward the edges of the screen. Two on screen at
most, one new one every 9 seconds.

Height is set to 300px in CSS (.dancer.sprite img) — change
that if your art wants to be bigger or smaller.

A note on what to use: use artwork you have the rights to.
Commissioned art, art you made, or assets licensed for use are
all fine. Well known characters from anime you like are
somebody else's property, so they are not something to ship,
though obviously nothing stops you using them locally.
